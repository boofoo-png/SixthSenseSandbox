User Research Plan: Marketing Dashboard

Screener Questions

Do you currently manage social media or marketing content for a business or organization?

Have you used marketing platforms such as Hootsuite, Sprout Social, or Metricool?

Have you experienced difficulties managing or analyzing marketing content?

Research Questions

Specific: Which features would you find most useful — content creation, scheduling, analytics, or competitor analysis?

Practical: What difficulties do you experience when managing marketing tasks across different platforms?

Actionable: What would make you consider switching from your current marketing platform to a different one?
